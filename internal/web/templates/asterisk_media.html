Technical media coverage and correlation

Current reports require an active reconciled channel, matching runtime and AMI session, no known event gap, and receipt within 60 seconds. Older reports remain historical. Endpoint joins use the channel's verified endpoint-definition association. The bounded report window may omit older streams. AMI session sequence prevents retry duplication within a session; no durable cross-session replay identity is supplied.
